Two Brothers, One Dream, Orlando Magic

Player Profiles

#22

Franz Wagner

Forward

24.2 PPG
5.7 RPG
4.7 APG

The younger Wagner brother has emerged as a cornerstone of the Magic franchise. Franz's versatility and two-way play make him one of the NBA's rising stars. In the 2024-25 season, he averaged a career-high 24.2 points per game, ranking 15th in the NBA in scoring. His ability to score, facilitate, and defend has established him as Orlando's go-to player in crucial moments.

#21

Moritz Wagner

Forward-Center

12.9 PPG
4.9 RPG
1.4 APG

The older Wagner brother brings energy, toughness, and veteran leadership to the Magic. Moritz's physical play and court presence provide invaluable support off the bench. During the 2024-25 season, he was enjoying a career-best start with 12.9 points per game on an impressive 56.2% shooting. His intensity and hustle make him a fan favorite and a crucial piece of Orlando's rotation.
