Season Preview

New Voice on the Bench

Orlando fired Jamahl Mosley in May after five seasons and hired Sean Sweeney, formerly an associate head coach with the San Antonio Spurs, as head coach on June 1, 2026.

Vučević Comes Home

Nikola Vučević re-signed with the Magic on a one-year deal, returning to the team where he had his most success and adding size, shooting, and experience at center.

A Quiet, Steady Offseason

Jonathan Isaac and Jevon Carter re-signed on minimum deals and the Magic added rookie Izaiyah Nelson (No. 51 pick) on a two-way deal, betting continuity and health can push a 45-37 team further than a first-round exit.
